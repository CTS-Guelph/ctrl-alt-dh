Abstract: This project explores the risks associated with children and adolescents sharing personal information on social media platforms, including privacy violations, cyberbullying, and the unintended consequences of oversharing. Alongside an informational article, this project features an interactive game designed to engage players in realistic online situations where they must make decisions about what information to share, how to respond to unfamiliar users, and how to navigate potentially unsafe interactions. By combining informational content with scenario-based gameplay, the project seeks to encourage safer and responsible social media practices through interactive learning.
