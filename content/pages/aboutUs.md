<h1 align="center">Welcome to CTRL+ALT+DH!</h1>

CTRL+ALT+DH is an online, student-run, open-access and peer-reviewed undergraduate journal from the [Culture and Technology Studies (CTS) program](https://www.uoguelph.ca/arts/cts) at the University of Guelph established in 2023. 

It is a journal that delves into the evolving relationship between culture and technology. Operated by the fourth-year [CTS 4000: Digital Publishing](https://calendar.uoguelph.ca/undergraduate-calendar/course-descriptions/cts/) class at the University of Guelph, this journal highlights diverse perspectives of students through research, multimedia projects, and creative works. Our goal is to integrate our contributors’ understanding of current frameworks from the perspective of the CTS program. The journal offers undergraduates a platform to critically engage with digital humanities and contribute to meaningful discussions on the societal impact of technology.

# Who We Are

The journal was named CTRL+ALT+DH to represent our focus on the relationship between digital humanities (DH) and online tools (CTRL+ALT).  
 
CTRL+ALT+DH is an online platform for CTS students to create dynamic and intellectual projects around digital humanities and share them with their fellow students. In publishing students’ works, we hope to provoke critical thinking, and inspire others to do the same while considering culture and technology studies. 
 
Everyone is welcome to engage and explore our existing publications and investigate the perspectives we CTS students have to offer.  All CTS undergraduate students are encouraged to submit their own projects for consideration to publish to the journal!  We publish multiple forms of media including games, art, creative writing, essays, and more.  From student assignments through CTS courses to curious creations analyzing society and media— our journal highlights the best of what students have to offer.  We hope to include your passions for technology and digital humanities in our next issue!
